Review the FarmBot demo

Explore FarmBot Genesis v1.8, identify a component and follow its documented parts reference.

  1. Open the assembled machine and inspect its arrangement.
  2. Choose CAD mode and open an assembly. Use Explode, Assemble and Reset.
  3. Select a component. Read its name, reference and fit qualifications. Isolate is available for a selected CAD occurrence.
  4. Use Find a part to search a name or number. Confirm configuration, kit, quantity and supplier applicability.
  5. Save a part and download a parts request if useful. Downloads can include your machine notes; review them before sharing. No request or order is sent automatically.

Record the component, browser/device, steps, expected result and observed result in your agreed review channel. Phone-sized browser review does not establish physical-device or OEM approval.
